    Why it works

    Built for how email really renders

    • Tables and inline styles

      The one layout every email client agrees on. No flexbox, no classes for Outlook to strip.

    • Hosted logo, not an attachment

      A PNG with a fixed size loads from the web, so it never arrives as a file.

    • Web-safe fonts

      Arial and Helvetica look the same everywhere. Web fonts fall back to Times in Outlook.

    • Under Gmail's limit

      Gmail refuses signatures over 10,000 characters. The meter keeps you inside it.

    • One tap on a phone

      Phone and email are real links: tap to call, tap to write.

    • Visible in dark mode

      The logo sits on a solid circle, so it doesn't vanish in a dark inbox.

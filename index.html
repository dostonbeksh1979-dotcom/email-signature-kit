How to install

Press Copy signature first, then follow the steps for your email app.

Gmail
  1. Open Gmail, click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new.
  3. Paste into the box (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V).
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose it for new emails and replies.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
Apple Mail (Mac)
  1. Open Mail, then Mail → Settings → Signatures.
  2. Select your account and click +.
  3. Untick Always match my default message font, otherwise Mail removes the formatting.
  4. Paste into the right-hand box and close the window.
  5. If the logo shows as a question mark, check that you're online: the logo loads from the web, so it isn't sent as an attachment.
iPhone and iPad
  1. Open this page on your iPhone and tap Copy signature.
  2. Go to Settings → Apps → Mail → Signature and paste.
  3. If the formatting disappears, shake the iPhone straight after pasting and tap Undo. The formatted version comes back.
Outlook (new Outlook and outlook.com)
  1. Open Settings → Account → Signatures.
  2. Click New signature, give it a name and paste.
  3. Choose it as the default for new messages and replies, then Save.

Why these signatures don't break

  • Tables and inline styles. It's the one layout method every email client agrees on. No flexbox, no CSS classes for Outlook to strip.
  • A hosted logo, not an attachment. The PNG loads from the web with a fixed size, so it doesn't arrive as a file or blow up on phones.
  • Web-safe fonts. Arial and Helvetica look the same everywhere; custom web fonts fall back to Times New Roman in Outlook.
  • Under Gmail's limit. Gmail refuses signatures over 10,000 characters. The counter above keeps you inside it.
  • Tap to call and tap to email. Phone and email are real links, so they work on a phone with one tap.
  • Visible in dark mode. The logo sits on a solid circle, so it doesn't disappear when the inbox turns dark.
